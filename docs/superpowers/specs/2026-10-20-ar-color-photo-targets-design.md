Preserve original bytes and offer full-frame color web copies with EXIF orientation, no recoloring or cropping. Validation: unique image/index coverage, original hashes, non-grayscale web pixels, compiled descriptors, print color and portrait orientation, all ten actual synthetic-camera recognitions, wrong-photo negative check, cleanup and production build.
