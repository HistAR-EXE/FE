# Hybrid AR center — approved option A

Goal: one consistent entry for desktop 3D, image-tracked AR at home, and existing onsite QR.

1. Inspect bundled image targets and recover matching printable target images; reuse lamp/hat models and optimize textures where possible.
2. Add a public /ar-experience page, shared artifact catalog, on-demand Three.js viewer, phone QR handoff, printable target mode and consent-first camera experience.
3. Keep /scan for onsite QR/GPS. Remove the old AR-to-checkin bridge; route legacy AR links to the new center and update navigation in both modes.
4. Use blue/yellow/light surfaces from the quest UI, responsive layout, explicit loading/error/unsupported states, camera cleanup and keyboard-accessible controls.
5. Verify meaningful navigation, camera lifecycle, origin/source validation, absence of reward/check-in writes, real model loading, responsive layout, lint and production build. Physical device tracking remains a separate manual verification.

Remote AR never calls onsite check-in or changes quest rewards. QR handoff shares only artifact and view, not authentication or location evidence.
